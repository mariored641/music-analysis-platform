# Étude score sources

The library catalogue links each score to its source. All bundled scores use compressed MusicXML.

## Frédéric Chopin

- **Op. 10 and Op. 25, all 24 études.** Maurice Schlesinger (Paris, 1833) and Henry Lemoine (Paris, 1837), respectively. Copyright 2017–2021 The Fryderyk Chopin Institute. *Chopin First Editions*, [Chopin Scores](https://chopinscores.org), [Humdrum encodings](https://github.com/pl-wnifc/humdrum-chopin-first-editions/tree/95dfb105c1669c72d10b04088566154f12d3dc1c), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Converted to MusicXML with converter21 4.0.2 and music21 10.5.2.
- **B.130 No. 1.** Romain Behar’s public-domain [Mutopia Project engraving no. 1895](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=1895), converted to MusicXML by [Artform](https://jamescarterpiano.com/sheet-music/chopin/trois-nouvelles-etudes-no-1-in-f-minor/).
- **B.130 Nos. 2 and 3.** Copyright © 2025 Knute Snortum, [LilyPond engravings](https://github.com/ksnortum/chopin-b130/tree/9f191991af80b71f23a52856eadea037c18d8fc8), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Converted with LilyPond 2.26 and [Sightread’s LilyPond exporter](https://sightread.dev/lilypond-to-musicxml).
